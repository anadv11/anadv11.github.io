The CARE and World Sufficiency Lab programme is a 6 month research project where students work together on sufficiency policies


## Course Objectives
- Gathering students from Sciences Po and partner Canadian universities under the CARE project
- Developing a policy brief and a presentation applying Sufficiency to policy analysis in France and Canada, focusing on food, healthcare, digitalisation, water and trade.

## Topics Covered
- Sufficiency policies and theory
- Research skills
- Team work and collaboration using Miro
